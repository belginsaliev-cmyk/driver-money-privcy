# Privacy Policy – Driver Money **Last updated: November 18, 2026** Driver Money is an application designed to help drivers manage their income, expenses, and financial calculations. ## Data Storage All income, expense, and financial records entered by the user are stored locally on the user's device. Driver Money does not transmit these financial records to external servers. ## Personal Information Driver Money does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use its core financial features. ## Data Sharing Driver Money does not sell or share users' locally stored financial records with third parties. ## Data Security Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their devices. Locally stored data may be removed when the application is uninstalled or its storage is cleared. ## Data Deletion Users can delete their locally stored financial information by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application. ## Changes to This Policy This privacy policy may be updated when the application's features or data practices change.
